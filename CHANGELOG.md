# Changelog

What changed in each version, as the app shows it once after updating. Newest first; each
version is a `## x.y.z` heading followed by `- ` bullets written for players.

## 0.12.0

- Easier to read everywhere: stronger contrast, clearer headings, and the current, next and refund points stand out from each other, in the Main window and the in-game panels.
- A sharper text font, bundled with the app, and drawn icons for gem marks and Guide steps, so they look the same on every system.
- The in-game panels are slightly more solid over the game.
- Fixed: re-syncing, or a refund being checked, while the app switched loadouts could put one character's points into another loadout.
- Fixed: the Gems section could stay on "Importing…".
- Fixed: Trade links could stay on Standard until restart when the league lookup failed.
- Fixed: a panel hotkey pressed right after switching to the game could be ignored.
- Smoother while playing: points ticking, zooming and dragging the Corner panel do less work, and the app saves less often.

## 0.11.3

- The tray menu is shorter: **Open POE Tracker**, **Settings…** and **Quit POE Tracker**. The sections, Loadouts and Re-sync are in the main window's sidebar.
- The in-game panels now open and close only with their hotkeys, not from the tray.

## 0.11.2

- The app now checks for updates while it's open, not only at launch. When a new version is out, a button in the sidebar (and an item in the tray menu) offers to update right away.
- After an update, this list of what's new shows once.

## 0.11.1

- The Build section lists your loadouts. Click one to follow it; Edit, Duplicate and Delete sit on each row.
- New loadout and Duplicate open an editor, and nothing is created until you Save.
- Every loadout has a name, and the one you're following is named in the sidebar on every section.
- A new loadout for a character the app already follows can keep the points it already tracks.

## 0.11.0

- Loadouts: keep several Character + Build pairings, even for the same character, and switch between them in the Build section or the tray.
- A character's taken points, league and Leveling guide step are shared by all its loadouts.
- When the game log shows another character with a loadout levelling up, the app switches to it by itself.

## 0.10.3

- Click the Ascendancy's header to fold it away when it covers the passive tree; click again to bring it back.

## 0.10.2

- Esc no longer hides any panel: each panel opens and closes only with its own hotkey or the tray.

## 0.10.1

- A clearer Zone layout pick in the Guide panel.

## 0.10.0

- Zone layouts and Directions in the Guide panel: pick the layout that matches your minimap and get arrows past the step's objectives to its exit.
